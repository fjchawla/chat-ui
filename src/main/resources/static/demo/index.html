What are the requirements for proactive service delivery in the UAE Government Services Code?
Proactive service delivery is addressed under Req. 2.3.1–2.3.4, which mandate that entities anticipate and initiate service delivery without requiring customer action. This includes automated eligibility notifications, pre-filled forms, and event-triggered outreach…

Amendment
Req. 1.3.2 — Updated Waiting Time Standards for Physical Service Centres
Mandatory benchmarks for maximum processing times across different service categories. Reducing Service Delivery Time Standards.
New Requirement
Req. 2.4.7 — Proactive Notification Standards for Service Status Updates
New requirement added under Digital Services domain mandating real-time status notifications for all transactional services.
Clarification
Domain 5 — Interpretation Guidance for Cybersecurity Compliance Requirements
Additional interpretation notes added to requirements 5.1.1–5.1.4 to clarify scope for shared-service entities.

New sub-category added covering requirements for using AI tools in citizen engagement and service co-design processes.
