What are the requirements for proactive service delivery in the UAE Government Services Code?
Proactive service delivery is addressed under Req. 2.3.1–2.3.4. Entities must anticipate and initiate service delivery without requiring customer action — including automated eligibility notifications, pre-filled forms, and event-triggered outreach based on life events or status changes.
📎 Source: Req. 2.3.1 – 2.3.4 · Domain 2
