Popular Questions

Frequently Asked

Common queries about the Emirates Code, answered by AI.

What are the mandatory requirements for digital services?
The Code mandates that all digital government services meet 47 core requirements across usability, accessibility, performance, and security. Services must achieve a minimum usability score of 80%, support Arabic and English interfaces, and comply with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
Digital Services Accessibility Usability 12 references
How should accessibility be implemented across channels?
Accessibility requirements apply universally across digital, physical, and telephone channels. Digital channels must conform to WCAG 2.1 AA, physical centers must meet universal design standards, and telephone services must provide relay services for hearing-impaired customers.
Accessibility Omnichannel Inclusion 8 references
How does the Emirates Code define proactive services?
Proactive services are defined as government services delivered to customers without requiring them to initiate a request. The Emirates Code requires entities to identify at least 20% of their services suitable for proactive delivery and implement life-event-based service triggers.
Proactive Services Customer Experience 6 references
What KPIs does the Code require entities to track?
Entities must track 15 mandatory KPIs including Customer Satisfaction Score (target: ≥4.5/5), First Contact Resolution Rate (target: ≥85%), Digital Adoption Rate (target: ≥70%), and Average Service Completion Time benchmarked against sector standards.
