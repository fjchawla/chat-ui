Popular Questions

Common questions answered directly from the Emirates Code knowledge base.

🖥️
What are the mandatory requirements for digital service portals?
6 source requirements
AI Answer Preview
Digital service portals must comply with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards, support both Arabic and English with full RTL layout, and integrate with UAE PASS for single sign-on. They must provide real-time service status updates and load within 3 seconds on standard mobile connections.
📌 Req. 2.1.1 — 2.1.6 · Digital Services Domain
📱
Are there specific requirements for mobile applications?
4 source requirements
AI Answer Preview
Mobile applications delivering government services must be available on both iOS and Android, achieve a minimum App Store rating of 4.0, support offline functionality for critical service steps, and pass annual third-party security audits. Push notifications must be opt-in and bilingual.
📌 Req. 2.3.1 — 2.3.4 · Digital Services Domain
♿
What does the Code require for people of determination?
8 source requirements
AI Answer Preview
All government services must provide dedicated queues or assisted service channels for people of determination, with a maximum wait time of 5 minutes. Digital channels must include screen reader compatibility, high-contrast display modes, keyboard-only navigation, and signed language video support for key services.
📌 Req. 3.1.1 — 3.1.8 · Accessibility Domain
⚡
How must proactive government services work?
5 source requirements
AI Answer Preview
Proactive services must be triggered by life events (birth, marriage, death, graduation) using cross-government data sharing with citizen consent. Entities must notify citizens of eligible services they haven't yet applied for and pre-fill forms using verified government data sources within 48 hours of a trigger event.
📌 Req. 4.2.1 — 4.2.5 · Proactive Services Domain
